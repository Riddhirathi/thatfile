One-time setup

The extension needs your permission to read your Drive files. Google asks every app to register first. It is free and takes about 5 minutes.

  1. Open Google Cloud Console and create a project.
  2. Search for Google Drive API and click Enable.
  3. Go to Google Auth Platform. Set up the consent screen as External, and add your own email under Audience → Test users.
  4. Under Clients, create a client of type Chrome Extension. For Item ID, paste:
  5. Copy the Client ID it gives you into manifest.json where it says PASTE_YOUR_CLIENT_ID.
  6. Go to chrome://extensions and click the reload icon on this extension.

Connect your Drive

Your files are read straight from Google into this browser. The AI model runs here too. Nothing is sent anywhere else.
